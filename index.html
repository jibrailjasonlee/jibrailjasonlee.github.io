The mechatronic loop

Robots work when the mechanics, electronics, and software are designed together. My projects practise each part of that loop.

Design

CAD and fabrication that fit the electronics and the load.

Rover-1 chassis and motor brackets, dead axle assembly, machined piston

Sense

Measuring the machine and the world around it.

Wheel encoders and an HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor on Rover-1

Control

Closed-loop firmware that holds a speed or follows a command.

PID speed control and encoder interrupts on an ESP32

Integrate

Power, wiring, and communication that make the pieces one system.

6 V and 5 V rails, WiFi teleoperation, servo pan-tilt camera mount
